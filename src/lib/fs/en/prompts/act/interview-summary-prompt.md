# Player Preferences Extraction

## Your Task

Read the provided interview exchange and extract the player's preferences into a structured digest. This digest will guide the Act Plot generation.

{{generalInstructions}}

## What to Extract

Identify and record the player's stated or implied preferences across these categories. For each, capture concrete signals from the interview (quoted phrases when possible).

- **Themes & Tone:** Recurring themes, emotional register, and desired atmosphere (e.g., serious, lighthearted, dark, adventurous).
- **Protagonist Archetype:** The kind of protagonist the player wants to play (broad archetype, personality traits, background).
- **Conflict Preferences:** The types of conflicts, stakes, and tensions that excite the player.
- **Incidents & Events:** Specific events, encounters, set-pieces, or story beats the player described wanting to see happen. Capture them as concrete beats, not vague themes.
- **Pacing & Scope:** Desired story length, density, and rhythm (including any target scene/word-count guidance discussed).
- **Ending Preferences:** What kind of ending the player would find satisfying (flavor, emotional resolution, ambiguity, etc.).
- **Explicit Constraints:** Anything the player wants enforced ("must include", "must not include", hard no's, deal-breakers).
- **Open Threads to Carry Forward:** _(Only when a Previous Act Summary is provided)_ Unresolved threads, carryover states, or continued character arcs the player expects to develop in this act.

If a category has no signal in the interview, write `None detected.` for that category — do not fabricate.

## Rules

- **Faithfulness:** Extract only what the player actually said or clearly implied. Name the underlying preference when signals are hedged or indirect, but do not enrich with your own preferences.
- **Completeness:** Do not silently drop a stated preference. If uncertain whether something is a preference, include it and label the uncertainty.
